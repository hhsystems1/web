About Helping Hands Solar

Education first, no pressure. A division of Helping Hands Systems, a family-run business in Victoria, Texas.

Why we built it this way

Most people get a solar price before they understand their own bill. We flipped that: you see your roof, your usage and your utility's rules first, then the options.

If solar isn't a good deal for your home, that's a useful answer, and we'll tell you. If it is, you'll know why, and what each number is based on.

Our role and our partners'

Helping Hands

Analysis, education, your proposal review and your point of contact from first question to activation.

Licensed partners

Design, installation, financing and service are handled by licensed partners. Your proposal names each one and what they're responsible for.
