Federal and state incentive rules changed for 2026 and differ for owned and third-party-owned systems. We confirm what applies to your system in your proposal rather than quoting a number here.

Cash purchase

You pay the installed price and own the system outright. Ongoing costs are small: monitoring, an occasional inverter replacement, and roof work that requires removing panels. Payback depends on your rate plan and production; your report shows its assumptions.

Solar loans

Ask for the cash price and the financed price. Many solar loans add a dealer fee that raises the price to get a lower rate. Compare APR, fees, term, total repaid, and whether the payment jumps if you don't make a lump-sum paydown.

Solar leases

A fixed monthly payment to use equipment someone else owns. Check the escalator (how much it rises each year), what's covered for maintenance, transfer rules if you sell, and your options at the end of the term.

Solar PPAs

You pay for each kWh the system produces, at a contract rate. You still pay the utility for anything solar doesn't cover. Compare the starting rate and escalator with your utility's rates, and check transfer and end-of-term terms.
