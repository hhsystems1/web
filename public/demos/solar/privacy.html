Privacy & terms

How we handle your bill and details, and what our estimates are and are not.

Your electric bill

Bills you upload are used only to prepare your analysis and proposal. They are not used for advertising and are not visible in our website analytics or session recordings. Ask us any time to delete them.

What we collect

  • What you type into the analyzer and forms: address, usage, goals, contact details.
  • Basic site analytics (pages visited, buttons used) without your bill or account details.

Estimates are not offers

Numbers in the analyzer and reports are planning estimates from typical production for your area, the roof details you confirm, and the usage you give. Actual production, savings and price depend on a site review, final design, equipment, your utility's rules and financing approval.

Calls and texts

We contact you only about your request. If you opt in to texts, message and data rates may apply; reply STOP to opt out. Consent to texts is never a condition of service.

Maps

Satellite imagery: Esri World Imagery and its contributors. Address search: OpenStreetMap Nominatim, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Imagery can be out of date, so a person confirms your roof before any proposal.
