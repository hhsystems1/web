How it works

From your first analysis to the day the system turns on, and who handles each step.

  1. You + Helping Hands

    Analysis

    Map your roof, add your bill and pick your goals. You get a layout and a plain read of your utility plan.

  2. You + Helping Hands

    Consultation

    A call or visit to answer questions and check anything the tool flagged: shade, roof condition, panel upgrades, loads to back up.

  3. Design team

    Design & proposal

    A reviewed design with the exact equipment, price, payment options, warranties and who handles each stage.

  4. City + utility

    Approvals

    Permits and the utility interconnection application. Usually the longest wait; we keep you posted.

  5. Licensed installer

    Installation

    Usually one to three days on site, depending on system size and whether a battery or generator is included.

  6. Utility + service partner

    Activation & support

    Inspection, permission to operate, monitoring set up, and a named contact for service after.

Start with step one

The analysis is free and takes about 5 minutes.
