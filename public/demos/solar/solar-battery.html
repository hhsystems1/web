Backup power

Essential circuits or the whole home?

Essential-load backup

A small panel with the circuits you can't go without: fridge, lights, internet, outlets, maybe a well pump. Lower cost; the battery lasts much longer per charge.

Whole-home backup

Everything stays on, including large loads like central AC, electric ranges or pool pumps. Usually needs more batteries and a careful look at startup current.

Sizing in plain words

Capacity (kWh) is how long the battery lasts. Power (kW) is how much it can run at once. A large AC unit can need more power to start than one battery provides, so which loads you back up gets reviewed before any proposal.

Solar + battery + generator

For outages longer than a day or two

Batteries recharge from solar, but cloudy days and storms limit that. A standby generator (natural gas or propane) can recharge the battery or run the home directly when outages run long.

  • Fuel: natural gas line or a propane tank sized for your runtime
  • Maintenance: regular exercise cycles and yearly service
  • Integration: the generator, battery and transfer switch must be planned together

Planning it together

We map which loads matter, how long you want to ride out an outage, and whether a generator is worth it for your area. Generac-style standby units are one common path; your proposal names the exact equipment.
