FAQ

Good questions.

Still wondering? Call (844) 733-2597, Monday–Friday 8am–4pm.

What areas can you coat?

Pool decks, patios and porches, driveways and walkways, garage floors and steps, plus commercial entryways, walkways and outdoor common areas.

How long does installation take?

Many projects are done in as little as a day. Rubber Stone and VUBA Stone installs average one to two days, depending on size and the condition of the surface.

Do you have to tear out my concrete or counters?

No. We resurface what you already have. Minor cracks are covered and stabilized; if there is real structural damage, we deal with it during prep.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. 24 months same as cash, with longer plans and low fixed payments after that. Subject to credit approval. See financing.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes: offices, apartments, schools and retail. See commercial work.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We're licensed, bonded and insured (ROC 316055, BurWood, LLC).
