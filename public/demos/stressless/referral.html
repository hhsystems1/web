Referral program

Earn $500 for every referral.

Love your StressLess project? Share it with friends, family, neighbors and coworkers, and we'll thank you with $500 when they move forward.

How it works

  1. Refer someoneA friend, family member, neighbor or coworker.
  2. They book a free consultationIn-home, no obligation.
  3. You get $500Paid after they sign a qualifying remodeling agreement.

No limit: the more homeowners you refer, the more you earn.

Submit a referral

Qualifying projects

RubberStone® pool decks & patios

Worn or cracked concrete turned into a slip-resistant outdoor space.

VUBA resin pool decks & patios

Decorative resin stone that is durable and UV-resistant.

Kitchen countertop overlays

A modern kitchen without a full countertop replacement.

Cabinet refacing

New doors and drawer fronts on the cabinet boxes you have.

Shower remodeling

A low-maintenance shower designed for everyday living.

Referral terms

  • The referral must be a new customer who has not previously received an estimate from StressLess Remodeling.
  • The reward is paid after the referred customer signs a qualifying remodeling agreement.
  • Applies to qualifying RubberStone, VUBA, kitchen countertop overlay, cabinet refacing and shower remodeling projects.
  • There is no limit to the number of referral rewards you can earn.
  • StressLess Remodeling may modify or discontinue the referral program at any time.
