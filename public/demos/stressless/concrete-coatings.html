Rubber Stone

Small chips of EPDM rubber blended with a strong polyurethane binder. It forms a seamless, flexible surface that moves with your concrete instead of cracking, and it is a LEED-certified recycled product.

  • Crack-resistant: flexes with the slab
  • Slip-resistant around pools and entries
  • Cooler underfoot than bare concrete
  • Low maintenance: a wash and a top coat every few years
  • StressLess is the No. 1 Rubber Stone dealer, three years running

VUBA Stone

A premium resin-bound system that started in East Yorkshire, UK, in 2009. Natural stones are bound in clear, UV-stable resin, so the stone shows through and the colour holds up under Phoenix, Tucson and Albuquerque sun.

  • 100% permeable: water drains through
  • Handles vehicle traffic and high-impact areas
  • Discourages weeds, moss and algae
  • Popular blends: Ipanema Beach, Poseidon, Manhattan
  • Installs average 1–2 days

Sierra Stone

Natural stone aggregates blended with industrial-grade epoxy into a seamless overlay. Resistant to chemicals, stains and everyday wear, and it works indoors and out.

  • Indoor and outdoor use
  • Two colour blends
  • Handles heat, UV and daily wear
  • Cleans with warm water and a mop

How long do concrete coatings last?

With routine cleaning and occasional maintenance, they last for many years. Rubber Stone and VUBA Stone carry a 6-year limited warranty outdoors, and VUBA Stone garage floors carry a lifetime warranty.

Do you have to remove existing concrete first?

No. All three systems bond to structurally sound concrete. Minor damage is covered and stabilized during prep.

How long does installation take?

Rubber Stone and VUBA Stone installations take an average of one to two days.
