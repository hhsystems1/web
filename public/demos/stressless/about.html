About StressLess

Remodeling without the nightmare.

Our mission: transform homes and improve lives, one stress-free project at a time.

Derek Wood

Derek has more than 15 years in the home renovation industry and has owned multiple Rubber Stone locations. He started StressLess Remodeling to make remodeling hassle-free and affordable, turning a disruptive process into a smooth one.

StressLess Remodeling was founded in 2023 around a simple idea: home renovation does not have to be a nightmare. Every decision has to pass the Win-Win-Win test: a win for the customer, a win for employees and a win for the company.

“Less Mess, Less Stress is not just our tagline. It is the standard we use when deciding how a project should be designed, communicated, and completed.”

Licensed, bonded & insured

We are licensed, bonded and insured for the work we do, so you don't have to stress.

ROC 316055

CR-61 Carpentry, Remodeling and Repairs (BurWood, LLC). This classification covers the scopes of both the commercial C-61 and residential R-61 Carpentry, Remodeling and Repairs licenses.

What it covers

For projects of $50,000 or less including labor and materials, all general remodeling, additions, replacements and repairs to existing structures. Electrical, plumbing, air conditioning and boiler work is subcontracted to appropriately licensed contractors.
