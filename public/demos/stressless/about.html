Derek Wood

Derek has more than 15 years in home renovation and has owned multiple Rubber Stone locations. He started StressLess to make remodeling hassle-free and affordable, turning a disruptive process into a smooth one.

Every decision at StressLess has to pass the Win-Win-Win test: a win for the customer, a win for employees and a win for the company.

“Less Mess, Less Stress is not just our tagline. It is the standard we use when deciding how a project should be designed, communicated, and completed.”
