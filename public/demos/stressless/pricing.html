Keeping it like new

Rubber Stone

An occasional wash, and a fresh top coat every few years.

VUBA Stone

Routine cleaning. The permeable surface discourages weeds, moss and algae.

Sierra Stone

Warm water and a mop, plus an epoxy refresh every few years.

Counters & showers
