Installation

Do you have to demolish my existing countertop?

No. Our slabs are custom-made to fit over your current countertop and bonded with specialized adhesives.

How long does a typical installation take?

A standard kitchen countertop and backsplash is usually done in one day. Rubber Stone and VUBA Stone installs average one to two days. Cabinet refacing usually takes 3 to 5 working days; larger projects 5 to 7.

Is it messy or disruptive?

Our process is designed to be clean and minimally disruptive because we usually do not remove existing structures. Most people keep using their home throughout.

Products

What are the advantages of your surfaces?

They resist heat, stains and scratches, the polished surface is easy to clean and safe for direct food prep, and there are no grout lines to scrub.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Residential customers get a lifetime limited warranty on our manufactured products; commercial customers get a 5-year material warranty. Rubber Stone and VUBA Stone carry a 6-year warranty outdoors, and VUBA Stone garage floors a lifetime warranty. Full coverage →

How do thinner countertops compare to traditional stone?

Our engineered stone surfaces are infused with a proprietary polymer resin that adds strength and seals them for long-lasting use.

Can your products be used in new construction or bathrooms?

Yes. They work for new countertops in homes and businesses, and throughout the bathroom: vanities, floors, walls and shower enclosures.

Where else can I use them?

Fireplaces, home offices, bars, recreation rooms, feature walls and floors. We also remodel spaces in boats and RVs. Specialty projects →

Concrete coatings

What areas can you coat?

Patios, driveways, garages, pool decks, walkways, steps, courtyards, outdoor kitchens and interior floors.

Do you have to remove the existing concrete?

No. About 90% of our Rubber Stone and VUBA Stone projects go over existing concrete that is cracked, stained, faded or pitted.

Is Rubber Stone really cooler?

It is not self-cooling, but it is cooler to the touch than almost any other surface. Hosing it down makes it cooler still. Cooler-to-the-touch instructions →

Process & pricing

Do you handle plumbing and electrical?

Our installers do not perform plumbing or electrical work directly, but we regularly work with licensed professionals in those trades.

Are there hidden costs?

No. The estimate you get at the start is the final cost you pay, with no hidden fees or bait-and-switch.

How can I see how it will look in my home?

We offer free in-home design consultations with material samples, plus virtual consultations and a visualizer tool.

Do you offer financing?
