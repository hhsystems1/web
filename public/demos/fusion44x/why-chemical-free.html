Why chemical-free

The problem with chlorine pools.

Chlorine keeps pools clean, but it comes with red eyes, dry skin, that pool smell, and a never-ending chemical routine.

What you’re dealing with now

The chlorine cycle

  • Skin: dryness, rashes and irritation
  • Eyes and lungs: stinging eyes and coughing, especially indoors
  • The smell: chloramines, formed when chlorine reacts with sweat and skin
  • The cost: shock, acid, algaecide and stabilizer, bought and dumped again and again
What changes

Fusion 44X breaks the cycle

No chlorine, no salt

Nothing is dosed into your pool. Charged hydrogen does the work.

Balanced 24/7

pH held between 7.2 and 7.6, the ideal range, without testing and dosing.

Algae & bacteria

Eliminated as water passes through your pump and filter.
