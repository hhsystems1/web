From one backyard pool to 17 patents.

Fusion 44X was invented by RJ (Rob) Wagner after more than 30 years of work on a safer way to treat pool water.

RJ Wagner

In 1991, Rob set out to find a safer, purer way to treat pool water. He moved to Arizona, did roof and home repairs to get by, and paid a pool technician, Dick Calaway, to teach him the trade. Within three months he knew pool systems inside out.

Then came the hard part. He taught himself chemical engineering and built prototype after prototype, but every version melted its power supply. After nearly 100 calls from the Yellow Pages, a NASA electrical engineer named Henry Richards agreed to help. Together they built a power supply that finally held.

His first real-world test site was his hairstylist’s backyard pool. For years he refined the electrolysis and the chemistry there until it worked. Seventeen patents later, Fusion 44X delivers chemical-free pool water to families across the country.
