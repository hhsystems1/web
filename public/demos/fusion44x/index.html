The problem

Red eyes. Itchy skin. Buckets of chemicals.

That “pool smell” is chloramines, made when chlorine reacts with sweat and skin. And keeping a chlorine pool balanced means testing, dosing and shocking, week after week.

  • Kids out of the pool with stinging eyes
  • Dry, irritated skin and bleached swimsuits
  • Shock, acid, algaecide and stabilizer on repeat

How it works

One device. Four steps. No chemicals in your pool.

Our patented Hydro-pH-Infusion™ process does what chlorine and pH chemicals used to do.

  1. A patented probe sits in a small chamber of pool acid, outside your pool.
  2. Electrolysis pulls out negatively charged hydrogen.
  3. Your pump and filter carry it through the water, eliminating algae and bacteria.
  4. Balanced water, held at pH 7.2–7.6: no irritation, no smell.

FAQ

Questions pool owners ask

Still unsure? Book a free 15-minute call or call (775) 600-5305.

Does it fit my pool or spa?

Yes. Fusion 44X retrofits into any pool or spa, working with your existing pump and filter. Saltwater pools must be drained before installing.

What maintenance does it need?

About 2 minutes a month. Refill the acid chamber twice a month (32% or stronger). The acid never goes into the pool, only the extracted ions. The probe is replaced roughly every 6 months.

Will I ever need chemicals?

Only in unusual cases: storms and other acts of nature, very high phosphates, or a service error. Those chemicals dissipate afterwards with Fusion 44X running.

Is it safe?

Yes. It runs on DC power to limit shock and electrical risk.

Is it legal in my state?

Yes. Fusion 44X meets pool regulations in all 50 states.

How is it installed?

By authorized installers. Installation instructions are provided on request: contact support@fusion44x.com.

Should I drain my pool first?

It’s recommended. Draining removes stabilizers like cyanuric acid that otherwise linger. Saltwater pools must be drained.

What if it isn’t right for me?

Every system has a 30-day money-back guarantee and a lifetime warranty. Your pump and filter must be in good working order to keep the warranty valid.
