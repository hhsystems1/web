Help

Questions, answered.

How fast do orders ship?

We offer 2–3 business day shipping on all orders. Processing times may vary, and shipping times are estimates based on carrier availability.

Can I return something?

Yes. You have 14 days from the day your package arrives to request a return. For a 100% refund the item has to be new and unworn, with all original tags and packaging.

How do I start a return?

Contact us with your order details at hisseywhiskey@gmail.com or through the contact page. Once the return is approved and received, we refund your original payment method.

Is shipping refunded on returns?

No. Shipping costs are non-refundable.

How can I pay?

Credit or debit card, PayPal, or offline payment.

Where do I pick my size or color?

Items that come in sizes or colors let you choose on the next step, right before checkout.

Will a sold-out style come back?

Ask us. Use the "Ask about a restock" button on any sold-out item, or send us a message.
